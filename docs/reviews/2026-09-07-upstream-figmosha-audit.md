# Figmosha 2 upstream behavior and service integration audit

Date: 2026-09-07. Verdict: selected typed alternatives are implemented, but full Figmosha behavioral parity and complete orchestration are not demonstrated. The feature map accounts for helper/parser names; it is not a 100% implementation or live-validation certificate.

- Separately observed local `code-kb/figmosha2` HEAD: `547cefb4c90abaa1da68db5455b921cbf3f8a5b9`; it matches the requested baseline.

- Existing tests were inspected as evidence of their assertions; they were not rerun for this report. Existing historical validation artifacts are identified separately from source inspection.
