## Safe Plan Requirements

The current plan incorporates the material revisions from these challenges. M01 must bind cleanup to owned bytes and durable intent, preserve edited or unverifiable metadata, and restore a moved visible target only when that target is absent. Audit-generation recovery tests already demonstrate useful identity-aware cleanup patterns; they do not establish that every retained-replacement consumer reclaims safely. Same-owner processes can still change an open inode after a check, so the plan correctly avoids claiming an OS isolation guarantee.

M02 must cover both native-attempt creation and absent-target publication, including interrupted child-directory creation. A durable intent cannot authorize adoption of an unrelated directory merely because its pathname matches. Quarantine must retain uncertain ownership while avoiding a global admission failure for unrelated environments.

M03 needs a common run-state mutation fence across resume, validation and application, while preserving prompt cancellation and the executor's retention of reservations until dispatched work settles. The operation tests provide strong counterevidence to proposals that release all reservations immediately on cancellation or replay an unknown invocation. Recovery budget authority must be explicit and bounded; it cannot manufacture a new target grant from an expired generation deadline.

M04 must retain the versioned proof needed by already-issued receipts before reclaiming directories or preparation records. The current lifecycle verifier still reads identity-bound attempt evidence. Historical signed partial-apply proof and active dependencies must remain pinned or migrate to durable versioned receipt authority. Operation-ID expiry and original authorization remain unchanged; longer evidence linkage must not permit redispatch.

M05 must round-trip the exact reviewed prepared profile, including added commands, recipe-use data and observation manifests. A contract/version fence controls new effects while keeping valid old signed validation/apply proof available for bounded reconciliation. M06 must authenticate observations from the actual reviewed execution and invoke consumption verification with the correct candidate or applied-target binding. A declaration, passing stdout object or caller-supplied report cannot become receipt authority.

M14 should deliver an explicit authenticated failed status through the existing token-bound, bounded evidence channel. Failed comparisons can provide hashes, differences and safe immutable artifact references without satisfying acceptance. Timeout, malformed/truncated receipt, missing artifacts and partial execution need distinct dispositions. An exception before complete observation must not manufacture a complete failed receipt for observations that never occurred.

## Meaningful Acceptance Checks

These are proposed checks, not executed results.

1. Exercise the supported prepare → CLI/args-file review → exact confirmation → registration path with complete recipe-use and observation data, including prepared commands. Reject altered fields and retain old signed partial-apply evidence under the version fence.
2. Use actual reviewed native command outputs to verify required consumption for candidate and applied validation. Reject stale, missing, forged and mismatched run/source/manifest/environment bindings; keep completion fail closed.
3. Interrupt each owned mkdir/identity/publication boundary, restart and reconcile, and preserve foreign preexisting or substituted directories. Include absent-target C4/C3 and existing-target C2.
4. Exercise more than 64 signed replacements, 128 native attempts, 32 owner preparations and 128 global preparations after eligible reclamation. Assert physical accounting, durable cleanup recovery and continued verification of pinned receipts; active, dependent and orphan records remain charged.
5. Interleave validate/cancel/resume through the real executor with a blocked native command. Assert durable cancellation epochs and run-state fences, and verify that the common guard does not prevent cancellation signaling.
6. Cross the generation deadline before and after effects. Prove zero-write preflight rejection is retryable, partial/unknown effects require reconciliation, and separately authorized bounded recovery can validate the existing published target without extending dispatch authority.
7. Produce a real visual mismatch and nonzero helper exit. Assert authenticated failed repair evidence and distinct timeout/tamper/truncation/missing-artifact outcomes. A repaired run receives new bound evidence; only passing checks satisfy acceptance.
